# Application Pitch

## RunTogether

**Motivation:** Friends who would enjoy running together often end up running separately because they do not know when their plans overlap, and coordinating a casual run can take more effort than running alone.

With **Share Runs**, runners can share runs they already plan to do, including details such as time, distance, pace, and location. Their friends can see these plans before they happen, helping them discover opportunities to run together that they might otherwise only find out about afterward. This lets runners make their plans visible to friends without having to reach out to everyone individually. If a friend's plan works for them, **Join a Run** lets them join directly, turning what started as an individual plan into a social run without requiring someone to organize a formal event or start a separate group conversation.

Not every run needs to be completely fixed before it is shared. With **Flexible Planning**, a runner can specify how flexible they are about details such as time, distance, or pace. If a friend's run almost works, they can suggest a specific adjustment within that flexibility, and the runner who created the plan can accept or reject it. This helps friends find a plan that works without requiring a long back and forth conversation or giving others direct control over someone's run.
